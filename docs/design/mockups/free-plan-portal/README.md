# Free plan portal mockup

A clickable concept of the vendor portal's Free plan state, made on 2026-10-01. Open
`free-plan-portal.html` in a browser. The toggles at the top switch between Free plan, Managed and
Pilot ended, and turn mixed plans on or off.

It is a design reference, not a build contract. The governing epic is AECI-1212, which records
the decisions this mockup reflects. The spec section comes from AECI-1213.

The screenshots are 1440px wide, numbered in viewing order. Prices are drafts.
