About

About Procore Project Management

Procore keeps a construction project's paperwork in one place. RFIs, submittals, drawings, meetings and daily logs live in the same record the whole team works from.

It runs in the browser and on phones in the field, and it connects to the accounting, scheduling and design tools a contractor already uses. Every item carries its history, so the team can see who asked, who answered and when.
