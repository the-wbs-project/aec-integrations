QuickBooks Online integration

Direction:→Outbound

Rental Leak sends reviewed vendor rental invoices to QuickBooks Online as Bills, with the invoice file attached. It also reads QuickBooks vendor, customer and project data on demand for matching, and reads back the status of Bills created by Rental Leak.
