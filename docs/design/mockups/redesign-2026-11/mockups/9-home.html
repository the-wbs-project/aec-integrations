Why this exists

The way AEC firms find software is broken

AEC firms make six and seven figure software decisions on platforms shaped by pay-to-play rankings and vendor-funded visibility. The tools at the top are often the ones that paid the most, not the ones that fit the work.

Meanwhile the information that actually decides a purchase is hard to find: whether an integration really works, and what implementation takes once the sales pitch ends.

What we do instead

  • Every connection is documented

    Each integration records what it links, the mechanism behind it, and where it falls short. A logo count tells you none of that.

  • Position is earned, never bought

    Rankings are algorithmic. Listings are never boosted by spend, and there is no pay-for-placement.

  • Organized the way AEC works

    Browse by AEC category, project phase and role, not a generic software taxonomy with construction added as an afterthought.
