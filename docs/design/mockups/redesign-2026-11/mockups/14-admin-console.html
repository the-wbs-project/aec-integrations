The console table and the status chips

One table recipe and one chip tone mapping for every admin screen. The rows below are the proposal. Every status string is the one the code prints today.

One console table recipe

The console table recipe
PartRule
WrapperA region that scrolls sideways: role="region", aria-labelledby on the caption, tabindex="0". No shadow, no radius, no fill of its own, in a panel or on the page.
CaptionA visually hidden <caption> with an id. The visible h2 or h3 above it names the section.
Header rowThe Label role (13px, 600, sentence case, secondary ink). A hairline above, a strong rule below. Not sticky.
RowsHairlines between rows, no zebra. Hover and focus-within take the muted fill.
Identity cellth scope="row", weight 600, the link to the record. A second line in 13px secondary for the slug or email.
NumbersEnd-aligned, tabular figures, no wrap. Dates are absolute, medium form.
Absent valueAn aria-hidden en dash beside visually hidden words.
Row actionsThe row's one disclosure leads the row as a 44px icon button. Decisions live inside the expanded row, never in the collapsed one. A table with direct actions puts them last, end-aligned, as compact secondary or ghost buttons. Danger only on the confirm.
SelectedThe Forest-soft fill plus a ticked checkbox. The checkbox is the signal, not the fill.
ExpandedThe row and its detail row share the raised fill, with a strong rule under the pair. The chevron turns and aria-expanded is true.
DensityCompact: 8px cells, 40px text rows, 45px rows with a 44px control. Comfortable: 12px cells, 49px and 52px.
PagingPrevious and Next under the table with "Page n of m · N rows", for feeds and lookup screens (ADMIN_PANEL_SPEC.md §5.7). /admin/vendors keeps scroll-append.
EmptyNo table at all. One line of text where the table was, the filters stay.

Findings for the build

  1. The review queue is a table here. DESIGN.md says moderation queues stay cards because a review body is long. The table keeps the body one click away in the expanded row. Chris decides.
  2. The review queue loads a capped set today, with a note. The mock-up shows Previous and Next, as ADMIN_PANEL_SPEC.md §5.7 rules for feeds and lookup screens.
  3. A sticky header cannot work inside a table that scrolls sideways, because the region has no vertical scroll. The recipe drops it.
  4. Operations has 15 entries. The mock-up splits its panel into two labelled columns, "Queues" and "Accounts and site". The code has one flat list.
  5. Two labels are in Title Case today: "View Page" and "Audit Trail". The mock-up prints them in sentence case.
  6. Slot 2 of .aec-charts (orange, bot page views) falls below 3:1 on sunken and Bone. Charts sit on white or raised only.
