# Redesign mock-ups, November 2026

Start with `PLAN.md`. It is the full plan and decision record for the empty pair page (AECI-1571),
a redesigned product details page and a new site design language, made between 2026-11-11 and
2026-11-13.

It is a design reference and a plan, not a build contract. Nothing here is built. Sample data,
product logos and counts in the mock-ups are invented.

`BUILD_PLAN.md` maps the plan to its Linear issues (AECI-1720 and the SEO epic AECI-1731), with
the model each issue suits and the order to run them.

Open any file in `mockups/` in a browser. The small "Options" control switches states and
alternates. The default of each control is the decision.

- `mockups/` holds the four final HTML mock-ups and the sources that build two of them.
- `screens/` held the screenshots the plan embeds. Chris archived them outside the repo on 2026-11-23, with the `baseline/` set.
- `records/` holds the 45 design clashes, Chris's rulings, the legal review and two design reviews.
- `mockups/9-home.html` is the home page (AECI-1789), awaiting Chris's approval. Its decision note is
  `records/9-home-notes.md` and its screenshots (`9-home-*.png`) are archived outside the repo.
- `mockups/10-browse.html` is the browse lists: `/products`, a term page, `/search` and `/categories`
  (AECI-1790), awaiting Chris's approval. It is generated by `mockups/src/10-build.py`. Its decision
  note is `records/10-directory-notes.md` and its screenshots (`10-browse-*.png`) are archived outside the repo.
- `mockups/11-vendor.html` (the vendor page) and `mockups/12-pair.html` (the pair page with integrations)
  are AECI-1791, awaiting Chris's approval. Both are generated by `mockups/src/11-build.py`. Their
  decision note is `records/11-vendor-and-pair-notes.md` and their screenshots (`11-*.png`, `12-*.png`) are archived outside the repo.
- `mockups/13-vendor-portal.html` is the vendor portal shell, its Overview, the company Profile form
  and the acceptance screen (AECI-1809, epic AECI-1729). Chris approved it on 2026-11-24 and AECI-1809
  built the shell from it (`STAGE_2_VENDOR_PORTAL_SPEC.md` §6.25). It is generated by
  `mockups/src/13p-build.py`. Opened on its own it shows a control strip and the page in a frame; with
  `?frame=1` it is the page alone, and every control is a URL parameter. Its pictures are outside the repo.
- `mockups/14-admin-console.html` is the admin console: the shell, the review queue, the vendor detail page and the
  overview (AECI-1813, epic AECI-1729). Chris approved it on 2026-11-24 and AECI-1813 built the shell and the shared
  console pieces from it. It is generated by `mockups/src/14a-build.py`. Its control strip switches the open choices,
  and its legend panel holds the console table recipe and the status chip tones.
