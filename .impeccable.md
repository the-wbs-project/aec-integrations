## Design Context

### Users

AECi's operational interfaces are used primarily by engineers and on-call operators who need to diagnose system health quickly. The language must also remain understandable to colleagues without observability or infrastructure expertise.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and practical. Explain what happened, why it matters, and what to do next without sounding alarmist or relying on unexplained technical shorthand.

### Aesthetic Direction

Operational and information-first. Prefer concise, scannable language that retains exact thresholds and time windows while translating specialist measures into plain English.

### Design Principles

- Write first for comprehension, then preserve the technical detail needed to act.
- Define specialist measures in place instead of assuming prior observability knowledge.
- Use precise, active language and avoid claims broader than the underlying signal supports.
- Make alert copy state the condition, impact, and next action where space permits.
- Keep terminology consistent across dashboards, alerts, and runbooks.
