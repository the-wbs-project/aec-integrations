## Design Context

### Users
AEC professionals — architects, structural engineers, MEP engineers, general contractors, estimators, project managers, owners/developers, subcontractors, facilities managers, and technology directors. They range from 5-person specialty trades to top-100 ENR firms. They're evaluating six- and seven-figure software decisions and are frustrated by pay-to-play review platforms (G2, Capterra) that prioritize vendor spend over honest information. They need trustworthy, unbiased data to make critical technology choices.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy, knowledgeable, honest.**

Voice is confident and direct — editorial, not marketing. Anti-vendor, pro-transparency. "No BS" energy. Speaks peer-to-peer with industry professionals, not down to them. The brand earns trust by saying what others won't.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Clean editorial.** Think respected industry publication, not SaaS landing page.

- **Tone**: Refined restraint. Substance over flash. Let the content breathe.
- **References**: The Economist, Monocle, Stripe's editorial pages — confident typography, generous whitespace, purposeful color.
- **Anti-references**: G2, Capterra, generic SaaS landing pages with gradient heroes and feature grids. Avoid anything that looks like it's selling. Avoid AI slop aesthetics (gradient text, glassmorphism, hero metrics with big numbers, identical card grids).
- **Theme**: Light mode only for marketing pages. Application UI supports dark mode.
- **Source of truth**: `docs/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` for brand colors and visual principles; `docs/STAGE_1_SPEC.md` §2a for implementation tokens and theme mechanics.

### Color Palette
Brand colors are accents that layer on neutral surfaces — they are not page backgrounds. Forest is the anchor brand color. Clay is rare and intentional (max ~5% of any screen). See `docs/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` for full role descriptions.

**Surfaces (neutral, not brand):**
- Light theme page background: `#FFFFFF`; raised `#FAFAFA`; sunken `#F4F4F5`
- Dark theme page background: `#0A0A0A`; raised `#18181B`; sunken `#09090B`

**Brand accents (light theme):**
- **Forest**: `#1E3A2F` — CTAs, links, headings
- **Forest hover**: `#2E5C45`
- **Clay**: `#E89668` — connector mark, primary CTA fills, key highlights only (large text / graphical only — fails AA for body)
- **Bone**: `#F5F2EA` — warm-tinted accent surface (hero bands, callouts) — **not** a page background

**Brand accents (dark theme — approved variants, not ad-hoc lightening):**
- **Forest dark**: `#4A8870` — CTAs, links
- **Forest dark hover**: `#5DA088`
- **Clay dark**: `#F0A887` — same usage restrictions as light Clay (brand policy)
- **Bone dark**: `#2A2520` — warm-tinted dark accent surface

**Text:**
- **Ink**: `#0F1419` — long-form body copy in light theme (Forest is too saturated for sustained reading)
- **Stone**: `#6B6B63` — secondary text, captions, labels

### Typography
Single sans-serif typeface. No serif fonts anywhere.

- **Typeface**: Inter (Google Fonts), system sans fallback
- **Weights**: 400 (regular) and 500 (medium) only. Never 600, 700, or 800.
- **Headings**: Forest color, medium weight
- **Body**: Ink color, regular weight, 1.6 line-height minimum

### Design Principles
1. **Substance over style** — Every design choice should serve clarity and trust. If it doesn't help the reader, remove it.
2. **Editorial, not commercial** — This is a publication that happens to be a product, not a product pretending to be content.
3. **Earned attention** — Don't shout. Use typography, whitespace, and restraint to draw focus where it matters.
4. **Industry-native** — Design for people who build things. Precision, reliability, no-nonsense. The visual language should feel like it belongs in AEC, not borrowed from Silicon Valley.
5. **Transparent by default** — The brand promise is honesty. The design should reflect that — no tricks, no dark patterns, no visual manipulation.
6. **Sentence case everywhere** — Headings, buttons, labels, table headers. Title Case reads as marketing; sentence case reads as editorial.
7. **Borders over shadows** — 0.5px borders separate surfaces. No box-shadows on cards or buttons. Shadows only on modals and dropdowns.
