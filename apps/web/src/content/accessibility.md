We want AEC Integrations to work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, screen magnification or other assistive technology.

## Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## How we test

We test each release with automated accessibility checks (axe). Before major releases we also work through a manual keyboard and screen-reader checklist. Automated tools find only some barriers, so the manual pass matters, and some pages may still have barriers we have not found.

## Report a barrier

If something on the site stops you from using it, email [support@aecintegrations.com](mailto:support@aecintegrations.com). Tell us the page address, what you were trying to do, and, if you know them, the browser and assistive technology you use.

We reply within 10 business days.
