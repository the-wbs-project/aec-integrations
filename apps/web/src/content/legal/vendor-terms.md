These vendor terms apply when you claim a company on AEC Integrations (the "service") and when you use the vendor portal for it. The service is operated by The WBS Project (the "operator", "we", "us"). These terms sit under our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Everything in the Terms of Service still applies to you. Where these terms say more about vendors, these terms apply. The [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement) covers buying and using Managed licenses.

## Accepting these terms

The claim form has a required box that reads: "I represent this company and accept the vendor terms." Ticking it is how you accept these terms.

When you claim, you do not have a seat yet. A seat is a person's access to a company's vendor account. So we record your acceptance against you as the claimant: your email address and your claim request. We also record the time, the version of these terms, and your IP address and browser. Once we approve the claim, the same acceptance is recorded against your seat.

## Who may claim

A claim is on a company, never on a single product. You may claim a company only if you work for it or are authorized to act for it. Use your work email address.

We check each claim before we approve it. We may look at your email domain, your LinkedIn profile, the company's existing seats and any competing claims. We may decline a claim. If the company already has seats, its owners decide whether to accept you. We step in if they have not answered within 7 days.

Every approved claim starts on Free. The first approved claimant for a company gets an owner seat.

## Truthfulness

Everything you tell us must be true and not misleading. That covers your claim, your company and product details, and anything you publish through the portal. Keep it current. If something you published becomes wrong, correct it.

Do not claim a company you do not represent. Do not misstate your role or your authority. The acceptable use rules in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) also apply. If you misrepresent yourself or your company, we may decline or end your claim, end any licenses, and keep or revoke seats.

## Your content and our license to publish it

You keep ownership of what you submit through the portal. Our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) grant us a license to host, moderate, reproduce, adapt and display content you submit. That license covers your portal content too.

You confirm that you have the right to submit what you submit. That includes logos, product names, screenshots and text.

## We may edit or remove content

Our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) let us add, edit, correct, reclassify or remove any listing or content. That right covers content you submit through the portal. Claiming a company does not take it away. How we handle corrections to a listing is set out in our [Listing Accuracy Policy](/legal/listing-accuracy).

## Plans never affect ranking or reviews

No plan changes where you rank or appear, or whether a review is published. That holds on Free, on Managed, and for anything else you pay us for. [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works) explains how listings are ordered.

## When Managed ends on a product

Some fields can only be edited while a product is on Managed. When Managed ends on that product, those fields are handed back to us. The text you wrote stays live. You lose the right to edit those fields. We may update them again, including from our own research. Nothing reverts and nothing is deleted.

The [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement) says when Managed ends.

## Seats and revocation

A seat is one person's access to a company's vendor account. An owner seat may also buy licenses, assign them, invite colleagues and change the billing email. A company may have several owners.

We may revoke a seat, for example for abuse or misrepresentation. Revoking a seat changes nothing in billing. The company's licenses, their assignments and their term dates stay as they were.

If we ban a company's vendor account, any paid subscription is canceled at the end of its current term. There is no refund.
