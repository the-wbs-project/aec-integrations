## Who "you" means

"You" means both the person who claims a company and the company itself. The person who ticks the box accepts these terms for themselves and on behalf of the company. If you do not have the authority to bind the company, do not claim. If you claim anyway, you are personally bound by these terms.

A "company" is a vendor or connector vendor listed on the service. A "seat" is one person's access to a company's vendor account. A "product" is a software product listed under a company. A plan belongs to a product, not to a company. "Free" is the plan every product starts on and costs nothing. "Managed" is the paid plan for a product, sold as licenses under the [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement). What each plan includes is described at [Plans](/docs/vendors/plans).

## Accepting these terms

The claim form has a required box that reads: "I represent this company and accept the vendor terms." Ticking it is how you accept these terms.

When you claim, you do not have a seat yet. So we record your acceptance against you as the claimant: your email address and your claim request. We also record the time, the version of these terms, and your IP address and browser. Once we approve the claim, the same acceptance is recorded against your seat.

We keep the acceptance record for as long as the company has a vendor account, and after that for as long as we need it as evidence. It is kept even if you later ask us to delete your account. Owner seats can see their company's acceptance records, without the IP address and browser.

## Who may claim

A claim is on a company, never on a single product. You may claim a company only if you work for it or are authorized to act for it. Use your work email address.

We check each claim before we approve it. We may look at your email domain, your LinkedIn profile, the company's existing seats and any competing claims. We may ask you for more information. We may decline a claim for any reason and we do not have to say why. If the company already has seats, its owners decide whether to accept you. We step in if they have not answered within 7 days.

Claiming a company costs nothing. Every approved claim starts all of the company's products on the Free plan. Plans are per product, not per company. The first approved claimant for a company gets an owner seat.

## Truthfulness

Everything you tell us must be true and not misleading. That covers your claim, your company and product details, and anything you publish through the portal. Keep it current. If something you published becomes wrong, correct it.

Do not claim a company you do not represent. Do not misstate your role or your authority. The acceptable use rules in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) also apply. If you misrepresent yourself or your company, we may decline or end your claim, end any licenses, and keep or revoke seats.

## When Managed ends on a product

Some fields can only be edited while a product is on Managed. When Managed ends on that product, you lose the right to edit those fields. The text you wrote stays live, exactly as you left it. The fields stay yours: we do not update them from our own research, and our catalog updates stop reaching them. A new license for that product gives you the edit right back. Nothing reverts and nothing is deleted.

The [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement) says when Managed ends.

## Seats and revocation

A seat is one person's access to a company's vendor account. An owner seat may also buy licenses, assign them, invite colleagues and change the billing email. A company may have several owners.

You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email address. Keep that mailbox secure. Everything done from your seat is treated as done by you and by the company. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your seat.

Owners are responsible for the people they invite. When a person leaves the company or should no longer have access, an owner must remove their seat. The company stays bound by what that person did from the seat until it is removed.

We may revoke a seat, for example for abuse or misrepresentation. Revoking a seat changes nothing in billing. The company's licenses, their assignments and their term dates stay as they were.

If we ban a company's vendor account, any paid subscription is canceled at the end of its current term. There is no refund.

## Ending your claim

You may give up a claim at any time by emailing us. We then remove the company's seats. The listing stays public, because listings are compiled from public information, and the content you published stays live under the license above. Open licenses are handled under the [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement).

We may end a company's vendor account, with or without notice, if you break these terms or the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), if we reasonably believe the claim was not genuine, or if we stop operating the vendor portal.

The sections on your content and our license, truthfulness, indemnity, disputes, and the general terms continue to apply after your claim ends.
