## Who "you" means

"You" means both the person who claims a company and the company itself. The person who ticks the box accepts these terms for themselves and on behalf of the company. If you do not have the authority to bind the company, do not claim. If you claim anyway, you are responsible for the loss your false claim causes, and we may treat the claim as void and remove the seats it created.

A "company" is a vendor or connector vendor listed on the service. A connector vendor is a company whose product connects other products. An integration is a listing of how two products connect. A "seat" is one person's access to a company's vendor account. A "product" is a software product listed under a company. A plan belongs to a product, not to a company. "Free" is the plan every product starts on and costs nothing. "Managed" is the paid plan for a product, sold as subscriptions under the [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement). What each plan includes is described at [Plans](/docs/vendors/plans).

## Accepting these terms

The claim form has a required box that reads: "I have read and accept the Vendor Terms on behalf of the company named above, and I am authorized to bind that company." Ticking it is how you accept these terms. The [Listing Accuracy Policy](/legal/listing-accuracy) and the [Review Guidelines](/legal/review-guidelines), each as numbered and dated at those links, are part of these terms and bind you as if set out in full. The form names their versions beside the box.

When you claim, you do not have a seat yet. So we record your acceptance against you as the claimant: your name, your title, the company's legal name, your email address and your claim request. We also record the time, the version of these terms and of the two policies, and your IP address and browser. Once we approve the claim, the same acceptance is recorded against your seat.

We keep the acceptance record for as long as the company has a vendor account, and after that for as long as we need it as evidence. Your email address, the time and the version are kept even if you later ask us to delete your account. The IP address and browser are deleted 90 days after acceptance. Owner seats can see their company's acceptance records, without the IP address and browser.

## Who may claim

A claim is on a company, never on a single product. You may claim a company only if you work for it or are authorized to act for it. Use your work email address.

We check each claim before we approve it. We may look at your email domain, your LinkedIn profile, the company's existing seats and any competing claims. We may ask you for more information. We may decline a claim for any reason and we do not have to say why. If the company already has seats, its owners decide whether to accept you. We step in if they have not answered within 7 days.

Claiming a company costs nothing. Every approved claim starts all of the company's products on the Free plan. Plans are per product, not per company. The first approved claimant for a company gets an owner seat.

## When Managed ends on a product

Some fields can only be edited while a product is on Managed. When Managed ends on that product, you lose the right to edit those fields. The text you wrote stays live, exactly as you left it. The fields stay yours: we do not update them from our own research, and our catalog updates stop reaching them. A new subscription for that product gives you the edit right back. Nothing reverts and nothing is deleted. The public page marks those fields as maintained by you until the date Managed ended. For 30 days after that date, an owner seat may ask us to remove the text you wrote in those fields, and we blank them. Catalog data and reviews are not affected. The one exception to all of this is a suspension or termination under "Seats and revocation" below.

The [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement) says when Managed ends.

## Seats and revocation

A seat is one person's access to a company's vendor account. An owner seat may also buy subscriptions, assign them, invite colleagues, and change the billing email and the company's legal name. A company may have several owners.

You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email address. Keep that mailbox secure. Everything done from your seat is treated as done by you and by the company. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your seat.

Owners are responsible for the people they invite. When a person leaves the company or should no longer have access, an owner must remove their seat. The company stays bound by what that person did from the seat until it is removed.

We may revoke a seat, for example for abuse or misrepresentation. Revoking a seat changes nothing in billing. The company's subscriptions, their assignments and their term dates stay as they were.

If we reasonably believe a company has broken these terms or the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), we may suspend its seats and editing at once, then write to it naming the rule and the evidence, with 7 business days from receipt to fix the breach or show us we are wrong. If it does not, we end its subscriptions and cancel its billing, with a refund of the unused term less the fees for the period used and our documented cost, as the [Managed plan agreement](/legal/managed-plan-agreement) says. We may then correct or remove the fields the company maintained. The company keeps ownership of what it wrote.

We may end a company's vendor account after the notice described under Seats and revocation if you break these terms or the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), or if we find the claim was not genuine. In that case we give you a further 7 business days to prove your identity and authority first. We may also end every vendor account if we stop operating the vendor portal.

The sections on your content and our license, truthfulness, indemnity, disputes, and the general terms continue to apply after your claim ends.
