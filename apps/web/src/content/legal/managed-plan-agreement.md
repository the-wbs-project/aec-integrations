This agreement covers Managed licenses on AEC Integrations (the "service"). The service is operated by The WBS Project ("AECi", "we", "us"). "You" means the company that holds the licenses. This agreement sits under our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and our [Vendor Terms](/legal/vendor-terms). Both still apply.

You accept this agreement by ticking the required box on the Stripe checkout page: "I have read and accept the Managed plan agreement." We record the acceptance against your billing account and the seat that bought. We also record the time, the version of this agreement and the Stripe checkout session.

## What a license is and does

A license is one Managed slot. You assign it to one product. A product with a license assigned is on Managed. Its Managed fields unlock at once and its public page updates. A product with no license stays on Free. Several products under one bill can hold different plans.

Licenses belong to your company's billing account. Only an owner seat may buy licenses or assign them.

A license comes from one of three sources:

- a purchase through Stripe,
- an AECi-recorded grant, with a reference and an end date,
- a complimentary license, with an end date and no card.

## Prices and billing options

A license costs $250 a year or $25 a month. You choose annual or monthly when you buy. There are no volume tiers. A volume deal is an AECi-recorded grant.

Payment is by card through Stripe. A Stripe invoice is available on request. A discount, where we offer one, is applied at checkout.

If you add licenses during a billing period, Stripe charges a pro-rated amount for the rest of that period.

## Annual renewal

An annual license renews each year on its renewal date. It renews unless you opt out before the renewal date. To opt out, email us before that date. We then cancel the renewal in Stripe, and the licenses end on the renewal date.

We send written notice by email 30 days before the renewal date. The "Renewal notice" section below says what it contains.

An AECi-recorded grant or a complimentary license does not renew. It ends on its end date unless you replace it with a purchase. We send the same notice 30 days before that date.

## Monthly billing

Monthly billing is month to month, with no commitment. You may cancel any time, from the Stripe customer portal or by email to us. Your licenses run to the end of the month you have paid for.

## Refunds

We give a full refund if you ask within 14 days of the first charge. There is no refund after that.

## Licenses stay assigned until renewal

Once you assign a license to a product, it stays on that product until renewal. Unassigning it early is a request to AECi. We approve or decline each request.

You may flag a product "release license at renewal", for example a product you are retiring. At renewal its license returns to your pool.

If you renew fewer licenses than you have assigned products, choose before the renewal date which products keep one. If you do not choose, the oldest assignments keep their licenses, and we email you.

## When Managed ends

When a product's license ends, the product moves to Free. Its Managed fields are handed back to AECi. The text you wrote stays live. You lose the right to edit those fields. We may update them again. Nothing reverts and nothing is deleted.

## Suspension and non-payment

We may end licenses for abuse, for misrepresentation or for non-payment. In those cases we may keep or revoke seats under our [Vendor Terms](/legal/vendor-terms).

If a card payment fails, Stripe retries it on its own schedule and emails you. Managed stays on while Stripe retries. If Stripe cancels the subscription, the affected products move to Free.

If you file a chargeback, the affected products move to Free until it is settled.

If we ban your company's vendor account, your subscription is canceled at the end of its current term. There is no refund.

## Limit of liability

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us for licenses in the twelve months before the claim arose.

## Changes to this agreement

We may change this agreement. A new version binds you at your next renewal or your next purchase, whichever comes first. We announce it in the renewal notice sent 30 days before your renewal date. Nothing is re-signed mid-term.

## Plans never affect ranking or reviews

No plan changes where you rank or appear, or whether a review is published. A license changes what you can edit. It never changes the order of listings.

## Renewal notice

Thirty days before an annual renewal date, or before the end date of an AECi-recorded grant or a complimentary license, we email every seat and your billing email. The notice states:

- the renewal or end date,
- the price,
- the number of licenses,
- any change to this agreement that will bind at renewal,
- how to opt out or reduce.

Reducing your licenses, and unassigning a license early, are requests to AECi. Reply to the notice or email us before the date.

Monthly billing has no renewal notice. It continues until you cancel.
