AEC Integrations lists software products, the companies that make them, and the integrations between them. We compile every listing from public sources. If your company is listed, someone who works there can claim the listing and keep it accurate.

## What claiming is

A claim asks us for a seat on your company's vendor account. A seat is one person's access to the vendor portal. A person at AEC Integrations checks every claim before we grant a seat. Sending a claim is free and needs no account.

With a seat you can edit your company and product details, claim the integrations your company offers, add your own links, and ask for a change to an integration you do not own. [Claiming your vendor listing](/docs/vendors/claiming-your-listing) covers the form, what we check, and what happens next.

## Free and Managed at a glance

Every company with a seat is on one of two plans.

- **Free** costs nothing. You can edit your company details and each product's description, website, logo and categories. You can mark details as checked, claim and edit the integrations your company owns, add your own links, and add a new integration.
- **Managed** is a paid plan, arranged by invoice or purchase order. It adds the rest of each product's details, confirming data flows, integrations delivered through a connector, public replies to reviews, and telling search engines about your changes.

Confirming data flows means answering Yes or No for each type of data an integration page lists. The portal calls each of those rows a data flow.

[Plans](/docs/vendors/plans) in the vendor guide has the full list, and what happens when a plan ends.

## What no plan changes

No plan changes where you rank or appear, or whether a review is published. Search order is worked out the same way for every listing: first how well it matches the search, then how complete it is. Every detail that counts toward completeness is editable on Free. [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works) sets out the rule.

## The vendor guide

Once you have a seat, the [vendor guide](/docs/vendors) covers the rest: your seat and your colleagues, confirming data flows, the integrations you own, contests, replies to reviews, plans, your change history, and connector products.
