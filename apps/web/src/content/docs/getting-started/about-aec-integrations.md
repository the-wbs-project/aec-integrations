AEC Integrations is a directory of the software used in architecture, engineering and construction, and of the integrations between those products. It is free to read. You do not need an account to browse, search or read any page.

## What you will find

- **Products.** Each product is classified four ways: what it does, who uses it, which project phases it supports, and which trades it serves. See [How listings are classified](/docs/getting-started/taxonomy).
- **Vendors.** The companies that make the products.
- **Integrations.** Each pair of products that work together has its own page. It shows how they connect, which types of data move between them, and in which direction. See [Reading an integration page](/docs/getting-started/reading-an-integration-page).
- **Reviews.** People who have used a product can review it. Each review scores the product and the onboarding separately, as "Overall" and "Onboarding".

## Where the listings come from

Every listing starts from public sources, such as vendor documentation, API references, integration marketplaces and vendor websites. AEC Integrations compiles that material and curates it before it goes on the site.

A vendor can then claim its listing and keep its own details up to date. An integration belongs to the vendor that offers it, and that vendor can claim it too. The top of each page says who keeps it up to date: "AEC Integrations maintained" or "Vendor maintained".

AEC Integrations is built and run by The WBS Project.

## Two companies, two answers

An integration has a company at each end. On an integration page, each type of data shows which of the two companies has confirmed it. "Confirmed by both companies" appears only when two different companies have each confirmed it. Our own record never counts as a confirmation.

Most data today reads "Listed by AEC Integrations". That means we recorded it from public sources and neither company has confirmed it yet. [Agreement states](/docs/trust/agreement-states) explains every label.

## What is never for sale

No payment changes where a product or vendor appears, in search or in any list. Correcting a listing is free for anyone. [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works) explains what does decide the order.

## If something is wrong

Every product and vendor page has a **Suggest a correction** button. You do not need an account. See [Requests and corrections](/docs/reviewers/requests-and-corrections).
