Anyone can ask us to add something or fix something. It is free. It needs no account, only an email address so we can reply. Vendors with a seat on a vendor account have one more route for integrations, a change request, described at the end.

## Request a missing integration

If an integration you rely on is not listed, tell us through **Suggest a correction** on either product's page, or by email. Say which two products it connects and, where you can, link a public page that describes it. We add an integration only when both of its products are listed.

## Correct a listing

Every product and vendor page has a **Suggest a correction** button. Tell us:

- what is wrong,
- what it should say, and
- a public source, where you have one.

We check it against public sources and update the listing where the evidence supports it. We may decline or hold a request we cannot verify. You can also email [support@aecintegrations.com](mailto:support@aecintegrations.com).

**If the integration has been claimed by its owner,** the owner keeps its details and our catalogue updates no longer reach it. Send us the correction anyway. We check it against public sources. If it holds up, we ask the owner to change it. The owner decides.

## Ask to change an integration detail (vendors)

A vendor whose product is at one end of an integration, and which does not own it, can ask to change one detail from its vendor account. It works on the Free plan. In the vendor portal, open the integration under your product. Choose **Request a change** beside the detail, or **Request a correction** under Change requests. On an integration delivered through a connector, choose **Contest a field** in the Connectors section of your product's Integrations tab. Name the field, give the value you believe is right, and say why.

Who decides:

- If the owner has claimed the integration, the owner decides.
- If it has not been claimed, AEC Integrations decides.
- A request on the Owner field always goes to AEC Integrations, even after a claim.
- On an integration delivered through a connector, the owner decides only while it is on the Managed plan. Otherwise AEC Integrations decides.

A request usually stays with whoever was deciding when you sent it. In a few cases it moves to AEC Integrations, for example when the owner's Managed plan ends. [Contests and protests](/docs/vendors/contests-and-protests) lists them. You can follow it in Messages under Field contests, where it shows "With the owner" or "With AEC Integrations" while it is open.

While it is open, the public page keeps the value on record and does not show that a request exists.

How it ends:

- **Accepted.** The proposed value replaces the one on record. When AEC Integrations accepts one on an integration nobody has claimed, the change usually arrives with the next catalogue update.
- **Declined.** The value on record stays. You are told, with any note the decider added. If the owner declined it, you can ask AEC Integrations to review the decision within 30 days, from the request in Messages. You can also ask once the owner has left the request unanswered for 30 days, and then you have 30 days more. The owner can reply once. AEC Integrations says which side it agrees with. Its view is advice: the owner keeps the integration's details, so the value on record stays unless the owner changes it. If AEC Integrations agrees with the owner, you cannot ask about that field again for 90 days unless its value changes. Nothing about a review is public.
- **Withdrawn.** You can withdraw your own open request at any time. A request also closes as withdrawn if the owner or AEC Integrations retires the integration, and restoring it does not reopen it.

You can have one open request per field on each integration. To send a different value, withdraw the open one first.

## Reviews are separate

A concern about a review, not a listing, goes to [reviews@thewbsproject.com](mailto:reviews@thewbsproject.com) under the [Review Guidelines](/legal/review-guidelines).
