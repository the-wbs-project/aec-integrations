On an integration page, each type of data carries a label. The label says which of the two companies at the ends of the integration has confirmed it. You see the labels in the Detailed view, which is the default. [Reading an integration page](/docs/getting-started/reading-an-integration-page) shows where they sit.

There are six labels. Here is what each one tells you, and what to do with it.

## Listed by AEC Integrations

We recorded this from public sources. Neither company has confirmed it yet.

Most types of data start here, and most of the directory still reads this way. On an integration that runs through a Sync Service, the full label says that the company offering the integration has not confirmed it yet, because that company answers there, not the two at the ends. It is not a warning. Treat it as a well-sourced lead. If it matters to your decision, check it against the vendor's own documentation, or ask the vendor.

## A company says no

One of the two companies has said it is not accurate, and nobody has confirmed it. The label does not name the company. The company's reason is never shown on the public page.

The row stays on the page, but it is not counted in the "types of data shared" heading or on its card. It is not a disagreement between the companies, because nobody has confirmed it. Ask the vendors before you rely on it. The **Sources** button on the row shows which company said no.

## Confirmed by (a company)

One of the two companies has confirmed it. The other has not answered yet. The label names the company that confirmed. If we cannot name it, the label reads "Confirmed by one company".

The confirming company stands behind it. The other company has said nothing either way. If you rely on both ends, ask the other company too.

## Confirmed by (a company), which offers this integration

The integration runs through a Sync Service. A Sync Service sells, runs and supports finished integrations between two other companies' products. The company that offers this one built and runs it, so it is the company that confirms what moves. It has confirmed this row.

It is one company's word, the company that sells the integration. The two companies at the ends have not been asked, because neither of them built it. When the company that offers the integration finds a row is wrong, it removes the row from the page instead of marking it disputed.

## Confirmed by both companies

Two different companies have each confirmed it. This is the strongest label we show.

A company that makes both products still counts once. Its integration between its own products can read "Confirmed by" that company, never "Confirmed by both companies".

## Companies disagree

At least one company has confirmed it and another has said it is not accurate. We show both answers and do not take sides. It reports a disagreement between the companies, not a fault in either product.

Ask both vendors before you rely on it. The **Sources** button on the row shows who said what.

## Reading the Sources

Each row has an i button that opens **Sources**. It lists everyone who has answered:

- "Listed by AEC Integrations", for our own record,
- "(Company) confirms this",
- "(Company) says this is not accurate".

On a Sync Service integration, a confirmed row shows the company that offers it as "(Company) confirms this".

A closing line says what is missing, such as a company that has not answered yet. A company can give a reason when it says no. Reasons are never shown on the public page.

## What the labels are not

- **Not a test by us.** "Confirmed" is a statement by the companies. We do not test it.
- **Not a vote by us.** Our own record never counts as a confirmation. We cannot make a row read "Companies disagree" on our own.
- **Not set by a plan.** A label is worked out only from the answers of the companies that can answer: the two companies at the ends, or on a Sync Service integration the company that offers it. Their answers count the same on every plan. Any company with a seat can confirm or dispute a row on its own integration, on any plan. A row reads "Listed by AEC Integrations" when neither company has answered yet. [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works) covers what a plan does and does not change.
- **Not in search.** The labels appear on integration pages only.
