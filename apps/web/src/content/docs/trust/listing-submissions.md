AEC Integrations lists products, vendors and integrations compiled from public information. Anyone can suggest a product. This page says what we list, how we decide, and what happens next. Corrections and removals follow our [Listing Accuracy Policy](/legal/listing-accuracy), which this page does not repeat.

## How to suggest a product

If you work for the company, use the [listing request form](/vendor-program/list-your-product). It asks for the product name and its website. It also starts a claim, so it asks who you are. The claim dialog on the [vendor program page](/vendor-program) has the same form: choose **Claim your listing**, then **Not listed? Type it in**.

Anyone can email [support@aecintegrations.com](mailto:support@aecintegrations.com) instead. Give the product name and its website.

Either way, we reply within 2 business days.

## What we list

We list a product when all three of these are true:

- It has a live public website with a page about the product.
- A real legal entity stands behind it, and a public company register can confirm that entity.
- It does at least one thing for architecture, engineering or construction work, and we can describe that from public material.

Company age, revenue, beta status and company size do not matter.

We do not list products that are only a waitlist before launch. We do not list products with no public description. We do not list products we cannot place in our taxonomy.

If we decline, we say which condition was not met and what would change the answer.

## How a new listing reads

We write the listing from public material and place it in our [taxonomy](/docs/getting-started/taxonomy). Its page reads "AEC Integrations maintained". Once we have reviewed it, the page also shows the date of that review.

Nothing on a listing rates how mature a product is. The integrations it has, the reviews it has and the date of its last update speak for themselves.

## Categories and comparisons

We create the taxonomy terms. A vendor with a seat in the vendor portal places its own products under terms we have already created. A suggested placement that arrives with a submission is advice. We read it and place the product ourselves.

Where a placement looks wrong, we may ask the vendor for evidence. If none is given, we may reset it.

A listing never compares a product to another one. You will not read "a lighter alternative to" any product on a listing.

## Suggesting is not claiming

Suggesting a product needs no permission. It also proves nothing about who you are.

Editing a listing, replying to reviews or confirming integration data needs a claim, and we check each claim before we approve it. The For vendors guide covers this, under Claiming your listing.

Sometimes the usual checks are not enough. Then we ask for proof that you control the product's web domain. That is a small DNS record or a file on the website. We send the instructions when we need them.

## What no payment changes

No one pays to be listed, to move up a list, or to be named in a comparison. Plans buy the tools to maintain a listing, never its position. [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works) sets out what decides the order. The plans themselves are described on the [vendor program page](/vendor-program).

## Corrections

We fix our own errors at no charge, for anyone who reports them. A vendor who wants a listing changed asks through the vendor portal. The [Listing Accuracy Policy](/legal/listing-accuracy) has the full rules.
