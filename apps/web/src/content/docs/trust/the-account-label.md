Some vendor pages show a label that reads **"Active on AECi"**, with a **What this means** link beside it. This page is what that link is for.

## What it means

The label means the company has an active Managed plan with AEC Integrations. Managed is the paid plan for vendors. The label comes with the plan and goes when the plan ends. A company on the free plan does not show it.

## What it does not mean

The label is about the company's account, not about its software. It does not mean:

- that we have tested or rated the company's products,
- that its integrations are accurate or complete,
- that we endorse the company,
- that its listings rank higher. The label carries no weight in search or in any list. See [How ranking works](/docs/trust/how-ranking-works).

It is also separate from the agreement labels on an integration page. "Confirmed by" a company says that company stands behind one type of data on one integration. See [Agreement states](/docs/trust/agreement-states).

A company without the label can still look after its own listing. A vendor with a seat can edit its details on the free plan. The marker at the top of a page, "Vendor maintained" or "AEC Integrations maintained", tells you who keeps it up to date.

## Where you will see it

The label appears in one public place: the top of the company's own vendor page, under its name.

It does not appear on product pages, on integration pages or in search results. When you compare products, a vendor's plan tells you nothing about the software.

One other thing on the site follows the same account state. While the company's account is active, its vendor page and its product pages say "Already managed through an active vendor account". Their claim button then reads **Request access to this listing** instead of **Claim this listing**.
