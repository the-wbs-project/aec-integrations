Legal versions

Record a new version of the Vendor Terms, the Managed plan agreement, the Listing Accuracy Policy, the Review Guidelines, the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy before it takes effect. Publishing stores your answers, the summary and the PDF fingerprint. A material change is then emailed with the PDF attached: an agreement to every affected company, the Listing Accuracy Policy or the Review Guidelines to every claimed company, the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy to every account. The text itself is changed in the repository, not here. All times are UTC.
