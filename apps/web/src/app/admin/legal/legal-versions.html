Legal versions

Record a new version of the Vendor Terms or the Managed plan agreement before it takes effect. Publishing stores your answers, the summary and the PDF fingerprint. A material change is then emailed to every affected company with the PDF attached. The text itself is changed in the repository, not here. All times are UTC.
