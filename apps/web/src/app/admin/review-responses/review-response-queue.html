Review replies

A vendor that owns a product has answered one of its reviews. Nothing shows on the product page until you approve it. A reply may say a problem is fixed and invite the reviewer to add an update. Reject a reply that names or guesses at the reviewer, asks for a rating or favorable words, offers the reviewer anything, or reads as a sales pitch. Do not reject a reply only because it disagrees with the review.
